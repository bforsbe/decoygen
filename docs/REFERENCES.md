# References to cite (application note / paper draft)

Collected while working; check volume and page numbers against the journal before citing.

## Protein packing efficiency

Packing density = van der Waals volume / occupied volume (Voronoi cell or probe-defined molecular volume).

- Richards FM (1974). The interpretation of protein structures: total volume, group volume
  distributions and packing density. *J Mol Biol* 82:1–14. — Voronoi cells; protein interior ~0.75.
- Chothia C (1975). Structural invariants in protein folding. *Nature* 254:304–308. — interiors packed
  like crystals of small organic molecules.
- Richards FM (1977). Areas, volumes, packing, and protein structure. *Annu Rev Biophys Bioeng* 6:151–176.
- Lee B, Richards FM (1971). The interpretation of protein structures: estimation of static
  accessibility. *J Mol Biol* 55:379–400. — rolling-probe accessible surface.
- Connolly ML (1983). Solvent-accessible surfaces of proteins and nucleic acids. *Science* 221:709–713.
  — molecular (solvent-excluded) surface, 1.4 A probe.
- Harpaz Y, Gerstein M, Chothia C (1994). Volume changes on protein folding. *Structure* 2:641–649.
- Gerstein M, Tsai J, Levitt M (1995). The volume of atoms on the protein surface: calculated from
  simulation, using Voronoi polyhedra. *J Mol Biol* 249:955–966. — closing surface cells with water.
- Tsai J, Taylor R, Chothia C, Gerstein M (1999). The packing density in proteins: standard radii and
  volumes. *J Mol Biol* 290:253–266. — standard atom radii and per-atom-type volumes.
- Tsai J, Gerstein M (2002). Calculations of protein volumes: sensitivity analysis and parameter
  database. *Bioinformatics* 18:985–995.
- Fleming PJ, Richards FM (2000). Protein packing: dependence on protein size, secondary structure and
  amino acid composition. *J Mol Biol* 299:487–498. — occluded-surface packing.
- Pattabiraman N, Ward KB, Fleming PJ (1995). Occluded molecular surface: analysis of protein packing.
  *J Mol Recognit* 8:334–344.
- Liang J, Dill KA (2001). Are proteins well-packed? *Biophys J* 81:751–766. — proteins pack like
  randomly packed spheres, with many small voids; void content grows with size.

## Cavities and pockets

- Kleywegt GJ, Jones TA (1994). Detection, delineation, measurement and display of cavities in
  macromolecular structures. *Acta Cryst D* 50:178–185. — VOIDOO.
- Liang J, Edelsbrunner H, Woodward C (1998). Anatomy of protein pockets and cavities: measurement of
  binding site geometry and implications for ligand design. *Protein Sci* 7:1884–1897. — alpha shapes.
- Dundas J, et al. (2006). CASTp: computed atlas of surface topography of proteins. *Nucleic Acids Res*
  34:W116–W118.

## Packing as a decoy discriminator

- Sheffler W, Baker D (2009). RosettaHoles: rapid assessment of protein core packing for structure
  prediction, refinement, design, and validation. *Protein Sci* 18:229–239. — native vs decoy models
  separated by void content (prediction decoys are under-packed).
- Deng H, Jia Y, Zhang Y (2016). 3DRobot: automated generation of diverse and well-packed protein
  structure decoys. *Bioinformatics* 32:378–387. — fold-level decoys built to native-like packing; the
  protein-model analogue of the problem decoygen's ladder faces at its upper rungs.
